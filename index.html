FAQ

Purpose: This website will remove the manual effort with remembering temperature variables for Lochaber and Cairngorm climbing areas. By logging live temperature data, users can make better assessment on Scottish Winter climbing routes for routes that require thaw/freeze cycles such as gullies, safety with loose routes that require consolidation, or building of Water Ice. The graphs will also detail wind speed and direction at each 4 hour interval (various weather stations are limited to temperature only).

What do the graphs show?

Recorded temperatures in °C over time, not forecasts. Roadside stations show air and road surface temperatures separately.

What does 15SSW mean?

Wind speed of approximately 15 mph, blowing from the south-southwest. Labels show average or reported sustained wind, not gusts. Gusts are shown where available in the latest conditions and chart hover information.

Why are some readings missing?

A station may be offline, stale or may not supply wind data. Blank readings are not zero. A wind label of 0 means the station reported zero wind; sensor accuracy is not independently verified.

How often are readings updated?

The collector is scheduled every four hours at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UK time. This preview is a fixed historical snapshot, not an automatically updated website. Collection times may differ from station observation times.

How do I explore the charts?

Choose a date range, hover over a reading for details, and switch wind labels on or off. Drag or scroll to zoom and double-click to reset.

Chart times shown in UTC (GMT).
